# What is not proved here

Lean proves exactly the statements written, under exactly the hypotheses written.

* Theorem 1 is proved on the explicit frame vectors and their Gram matrix; the Kendall shape
  sphere itself, and the claim that `M` is coordinate-free, are not formalized.
* Theorem 5's holonomy and index statements, and Corollary 6 (`χ(S²) = 2`), are not formalized.
  Remark 7 is proved as a statement about 2×2 matrices, not about the `ℤ₃` action on shape space.
* Theorem 10's orbit measurements (the figure-eight, the contour count, the `10⁻¹⁵` residuals)
  are numerical. Only the identity of the shifted ledger and its minimum are proved.
* Proposition 17 is proved in its key step (transverse directions lie in the kernel); the
  count `rank dΦ = N − 2` is not formalized.
* Propositions 13, 14, 16 and 18 (the conical intersection at unequal masses, the optic axes,
  the circle of rays, transient amplification) are numerical results.
