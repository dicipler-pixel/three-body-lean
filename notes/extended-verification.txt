EXTENDED VERIFICATION, ROUND TWO
================================
Everything below was run in this VM on 2026-11-23. Each item says what was checked, against what,
and what the check can and cannot establish. Following the statement-audit discipline: statement
fidelity and proof status are reported separately, and a check I ran myself is not called
independent of me -- it is independent of the author's scripts.

1. THIS REPOSITORY: AXIOM AUDIT OF EVERY THEOREM
   Method: the 46 `theorem` declarations discovered by verify.py's own counting rule were each
   submitted to `#print axioms` against a locally built ThreeBody.Basic (lean -o, system Mathlib).
   Result: 46 reports, all "depends on axioms: [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]"; nothing
   outside those three. Union over the fleet = the standard three. No sorryAx, no project axiom.
   This reproduces locally what scripts/verify.py does in CI, without needing lake.

2. THIS REPOSITORY: THE FOUR FALSE CONTROLS
   Method: each FalseControls/*.lean compiled against the built module.
   Result: all four FAIL, each with "unsolved goals" -- a mathematical failure, not an
   infrastructure one (verify.py rejects the latter). So the CI's claim "four deliberately false
   statements that must fail" holds on this replay, including the new unit_norm_needed control.

3. THE PEEL PROJECT: ITS LEAN CLAIMS, IN THIS KERNEL
   Method: `lean LoopWall/ExceptionalPoint.lean` and `LoopWall/ExceptionalPointDefective.lean`
   compiled here with the system toolchain; `#print axioms` on the three wall theorems.
   Result: both files compile, exit 0. wall_sq, wall_isNilpotent and wall_add_one_ne_zero each
   depend on [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound] only. So the peel's nilpotency claim is
   kernel-checked here, not just read.
   Name audit of the edited (C'): trace_W, det_W, wall_defective_wallR, wall_parabolic_wallR,
   charpoly_W_wall, wall_not_diagonalizable, wall_sq, wall_isNilpotent, wall_add_one_ne_zero all
   exist. wall_sq is not only in ExceptionalPointDefective.lean: the same name is declared in
   LoopWall/BridgeUnderdetermination.lean, which is worth knowing if the paper ever cites it by
   file.

5. NOT CHECKED, AND WHY
   T1 and T2 (OAM leakage under broken symmetry; vortex winding under random perturbations) depend
   on the report's own simulation and its RNG seed. Re-running the author's script would reproduce
   its output by construction and would not be a check; anything else would not reproduce those
   particular numbers. They are left as reported.
   The 5-colouring column of the knot table is unchecked: no SnapPy in this environment, and
   KnotInfo stores no colouring column.
   Nothing here establishes that the paper's prose says what its tables show; that is the separate
   statement-fidelity question, and for this repository it is audited in
   notes/remark7-reaudit.txt.
