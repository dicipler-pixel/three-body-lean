EDIT 1 - THE PEEL PAPER, section 2, item (C')  (results/light-bridge-paper.md, lines 90-96)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The existing text already cites v15 and already says "the peel wall is the complex member". The
one substantive defect: it quotes the principle as being about "every REAL traceless 2x2 matrix",
which the peel violates. Cayley-Hamilton gives T^2 = q(T) I with q(T) = (1/2) tr T^2 = -det T for a
traceless 2x2 matrix over any field of characteristic not 2, so the principle is field-independent
and the peel is an instance, not an analogue. Proposed replacement for the first sentence group:

  **(C') The wall is an exceptional point.** This is an instance of a principle stated in
  *Non-normality is Connection Energy* v15 §2 and carried as atlas **B199** ("one wall where the
  trace form vanishes"): every traceless 2x2 matrix T -- over R or over C, since Cayley-Hamilton
  gives T^2 = q(T)I with q(T) = 1/2 tr T^2 = -det T in either case -- has its Hermitian and skew
  halves deciding the sign of q, and when q = 0 with T != 0 the operator is a nonzero nilpotent, a
  single Jordan block, **and that is the wall**. The instances listed there are the spinning key,
  critical damping, the epicyclic generator of a circular orbit, Koide's relation and the Ramanujan
  bound; the circular-orbit generator is atlas **B177** and carries the computed constant
  sqrt(2 V'''(6)) = sqrt2/18, and **the peel wall is the complex member of the family**. That it is
  complex rather than real is the only thing that distinguishes it.

and then, after the existing display (tr W)^2 - 4 ~ 128 delta, insert one sentence of evidence:

  Independently of the Lean, at the wall (cosh(r/2) = sqrt2, sinh(r/2) = 1) the matrix
  W+1 = [[4i, 2 sqrt2 (-1+i) i], [2 sqrt2 (-1+i), -4i]] has tr(W+1) = det(W+1) = 0, so
  (W+1)^2 = 0 by Cayley-Hamilton, while W+1 != 0 -- the same three facts as `wall_sq`,
  `wall_isNilpotent` and `wall_add_one_ne_zero`, reached without them.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
-------------------------
- v15 is not in this workspace, so EDIT 2 is drafted against the quoted passages only; the card
  numbers cited (B199, B177, B13) are the continuous atlas's, and v15's own numbering may differ.
- The symmetric slice is why the three-body case is not a member: on real symmetric traceless
  2x2 matrices tr T^2 = 2(a^2 + b^2) >= 0, zero only at T = 0, so the null cone of the trace form
  meets that slice only at the origin. A symmetric family can collide without ever becoming
  defective, which is why its wall closes quadratically (this repository) and not as a square
  root (the peel, the ISCO). That sentence, if wanted, belongs in v15's scope note and is proved
  in one line.
