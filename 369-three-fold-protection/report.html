What survived, in one paragraph

The 3-6-9 rule is real when it is read as a statement about a three-fold symmetry with no fixed points. In the numbers, x mod 9 ∈ {0, 3, 6} is exactly "3 divides x" (Lean). In physics, the same condition does three proven or computed jobs.

  1. Angular momentum. In a medium with 3-fold or 9-fold rotational symmetry, the modes with ℓ ≡ 0, 3, 6 (mod 9) are exactly the ones a 120° turn leaves unchanged. That sector is closed under evolution: the leakage is 0.0 when the symmetry holds, and 1.8×10⁻² and 9.2×10⁻² when it is broken.
  2. Lattices. In any lattice with a fixed-point-free 120° symmetry, every shell count lands in {0, 3, 6} mod 9. This holds for the hexagonal lattice A₂, for Viazovska's E₈, and for the Leech lattice, where every count is ≡ 0 mod 9. The square and cubic lattices, which lack that symmetry, fail.
  3. Knotted filaments. A knotted filament carries a mod-3 charge, its Fox 3-colourings, which no continuous motion can change. The trefoil carries it; the Conway knot does not.

Each of the three has a fixed-point-free order-3 action at its core. That is the honest "Growl-Sieve-Filter". It is also the triangle in his own diagram (r³ = 1), the D₃ symmetry of his three-body shape sphere, and his own Lean theorem z3_isotropic.

What died, even after running the opposite:

  • the claim that only ℓ ∈ {0, 3, 6} mod 9 can be protected (ℓ = 1, 2, 4 are protected just as well);
  • the 2ᵃ·5ᵇ tool, which provably never lands in the class (Lean);
  • Solfeggio frequencies as physics (the residues swap under a change of units);
  • "Apollonian curvatures confined to {0, 3, 6}".

2. Colour: what his files actually hold

This comes from a full read of Colour Selects the Leader v1.1 (77 pages), the HTML, the code, Colour Studio v3.1, the Mixin explorer and audit, the deep-dive addon and the mixin-colors repo.

  • 3, 6, 9 are colours in Elizabeth's Mixin labels: Red 3, Yellow 6, Black 9, with White 1, Blue 7, Green 2, Magenta 8, and Brown 0 as a placeholder. The arithmetic around them is mod 10 and mod 5, not mod 9:
    • the complement d → 10 − d;
    • the quarter-turn d → 3d mod 10, with 3 → 9 → 7 → 1 and 6 → 8 → 4 → 2;
    • the quartic character mod 5.
    His own scope note: "arithmetic symmetry of this embedding, not a physical hue rotation law".
  • The angles: White 0°, Red 45°, Black 90°, Blue 135°, with secondaries at 22.5° steps. Their digit sums reduce to 9 because of the degree convention (S12). In turns or gradians the pattern changes.
  • The genuine three-fold structure in colour is Schrödinger's three fundamentals and the chromaticity triangle, with the white point at (1, 1, 1)/3, in §2 of his paper. Three cone classes give a 2-simplex. With equal weights its symmetry is S₃ = D₃, the same triangle group as the Galois triangle diagram and the shape sphere. Exner's unequal weights (43.33, 32.76, 1) break it, so the symmetric colour triangle is an idealisation, and its residue is the leftover of a broken D₃.
  • Lean: no Lean file in the colour set formalises label arithmetic.

Rebuild for colour. The real 3-6-9 content of colour is trichromacy, a 3-fold structure whose symmetric form is D₃. The mod-9 digit pattern of the angles is a property of the degree unit. The 3, 6, 9 labels are Elizabeth's naming, with mod-10 arithmetic. Nothing here is discredited. Each piece is placed where it belongs.

4. The four mathematicians, from their papers

  • Viazovska (arXiv:1603.04246, read in full). The magic function is built from E₄, E₆, E₂, j, and Γ(2) theta functions; the level is never 3. Every Eisenstein constant −2k/B_k is divisible by exactly 3^{v₃(k)+1} (von Staudt–Clausen). Hence:
    • E₈ counts 240σ₃(n) are ≡ 0 mod 3.
    • Θ_Leech = E₄³ − 720Δ ≡ 1 mod 9 coefficient by coefficient, so every Leech shell count is ≡ 0 mod 9 (this does not hold mod 27).
    Her two optimal packings are both 3-6-9 lattices in the exact sense of S4.
  • Piccirillo (arXiv:1808.02923, read in full). The Conway knot is not slice. She proved it through the knot trace X(K): she built K′ with the same trace and s(K′) = 2, whereas slice knots have s = 0, using an RBG link of three components. Her paper has no mod-3 statement. Against his tools: the Conway knot has Δ = 1 and det 1, so it has no 3-colourings and no Alexander or Levine–Tristram signal. His abelian invariants cannot see it, and the smooth obstruction needs the Rasmussen s-invariant, which comes from Khovanov homology.
  • Mirzakhani (Weil–Petersson volumes, the 2005 preprint of Invent. Math. 2007, read).
    • The volume polynomial has degree 3g−3+n.
    • The recursion runs on the complexity 3g + n.
    • The pants are S₀,₃.
    • Trivalence gives the number of curves ≡ n mod 3 (Lean pants_curves_mod3).
    The genuine bridge to spacetime: her recursion computes the JT-gravity path integral (Saad, Shenker and Stanford 2019; cited, not re-fetched).
  • Figueiredo (arXiv:2401.05483, NLSM ⊂ Tr(Φ³), with Arkani-Hamed, Cao, Dong and He, read in full). The cubic theory contains the non-linear sigma model through a shift δ on even–even and odd–odd variables. The organising rule there is parity (mod 2). The 3 is the cubic vertex, whose tree count is the same trivalent identity as Mirzakhani's pants (Lean cubic_tree_trivalent). Surfaceology itself began with Arkani-Hamed, Frost, Salvatori, Plamondon and Thomas (arXiv:2309.15913); she co-leads its extensions.

5. The rebuilt 3-6-9 rule

Three-fold protection rule. Let a group of order 3 act with no fixed points other than the trivial one. Then:

  1. the states it leaves unchanged form a sector closed under every symmetric evolution, which for angular momentum is ℓ ≡ 0, 3, 6 (mod 9);
  2. every orbit-counted quantity, such as a lattice shell count, is ≡ 0, 3 or 6 (mod 9);
  3. on knotted filaments, the matching invariant is the mod-3 colouring charge, which only reconnection changes.

Instances: the triangle D₃ (his diagram), the hexagonal lattice A₂, Viazovska's E₈ and Leech, the three-body shape sphere (his Lean z3_isotropic), trichromatic colour with symmetric weights, the trefoil, and the pants and cubic trees of Mirzakhani and surfaceology (trivalence).

Grades.

  • Items 1–2 are proved: the arithmetic and orbit lemmas are in Lean here, and the symmetry selection is standard.
  • The lattice and knot instances are computed and match known results.
  • The colour instance is a reading. The weights break the symmetry.
  • The physical test is open: a 3- or 9-fold photonic structure showing which OAM transitions occur.

Appendix B. Lean: SM369.lean (core Lean 4.24.0, kernel-checked, 14 theorems, no sorry, no axioms)

/-- The residue set {0,3,6} mod 9 is exactly "divisible by 3". -/
theorem mod9_036_iff_dvd3 (l : Nat) : (l % 9 = 0 ∨ l % 9 = 3 ∨ l % 9 = 6) ↔ l % 3 = 0 := by omega

/-- The same for integers (negative winding numbers included). -/
theorem int_mod9_036_iff_dvd3 (l : Int) : (l % 9 = 0 ∨ l % 9 = 3 ∨ l % 9 = 6) ↔ l % 3 = 0 := by omega

/-- {0,3,6} is closed under addition mod 9 (the order-3 subgroup of ℤ/9). -/
theorem class036_add (a b : Nat) (ha : a % 3 = 0) (hb : b % 3 = 0) :
    (a + b) % 9 = 0 ∨ (a + b) % 9 = 3 ∨ (a + b) % 9 = 6 := by omega

/-- The class is closed under multiplication by any integer factor. -/
theorem class036_mul (a k : Nat) (ha : a % 3 = 0) : (k * a) % 3 = 0 := by
  rw [Nat.mul_mod, ha]; simp

theorem mul_not_dvd3 (x y : Nat) (hx : x % 3 ≠ 0) (hy : y % 3 ≠ 0) : (x * y) % 3 ≠ 0 := by
  rw [Nat.mul_mod]
  have h1 : x % 3 < 3 := Nat.mod_lt _ (by decide)
  have h2 : y % 3 < 3 := Nat.mod_lt _ (by decide)
  generalize x % 3 = u at *
  generalize y % 3 = v at *
  match u, v, h1, h2, hx, hy with
  | 1, 1, _, _, _, _ => decide
  | 1, 2, _, _, _, _ => decide
  | 2, 1, _, _, _, _ => decide
  | 2, 2, _, _, _, _ => decide
  | 0, _, _, _, h, _ => exact absurd rfl h
  | _, 0, _, _, _, h => exact absurd rfl h
  | u+3, _, h, _, _, _ => omega
  | _, v+3, _, h, _, _ => omega

theorem pow_not_dvd3 (b a : Nat) (hb : b % 3 ≠ 0) : (b ^ a) % 3 ≠ 0 := by
  induction a with
  | zero => simp
  | succ n ih => rw [Nat.pow_succ]; exact mul_not_dvd3 _ _ ih hb

/-- The synthesis's own "2^a × 5^b factorization filter" can never land in {0,3,6} mod 9. -/
theorem two_five_never_036 (a b : Nat) :
    ¬ ((2 ^ a * 5 ^ b) % 9 = 0 ∨ (2 ^ a * 5 ^ b) % 9 = 3 ∨ (2 ^ a * 5 ^ b) % 9 = 6) := by
  rw [mod9_036_iff_dvd3]
  exact mul_not_dvd3 _ _ (pow_not_dvd3 2 a (by decide)) (pow_not_dvd3 5 b (by decide))

/-- Multiplication by ω = e^{2πi/3} on the Eisenstein lattice ℤ[ω], written in the basis {1, ω}:
    a + bω ↦ ω(a + bω) = −b + (a − b)ω.  It preserves the norm |a + bω|² = a² − ab + b². -/
theorem omega_preserves_norm (a b : Int) :
    (-b) * (-b) - (-b) * (a - b) + (a - b) * (a - b) = a * a - a * b + b * b := by
  simp only [Int.sub_mul, Int.mul_sub, Int.neg_mul, Int.mul_neg, Int.neg_neg]
  rw [Int.mul_comm b a]; omega

/-- ω has order 3: applying it three times returns every lattice point. -/
theorem omega_order3 (a b : Int) :
    let w := fun (p : Int × Int) => ((-p.2), p.1 - p.2)
    w (w (w (a, b))) = (a, b) := by
  simp only [Prod.mk.injEq]; constructor <;> omega

/-- ω fixes no lattice point except the origin, so it splits every nonzero shell into orbits of size exactly 3:
    every shell count of an ℤ[ω]-lattice is divisible by 3, i.e. lies in {0,3,6} mod 9. -/
theorem omega_no_fixed (a b : Int) (h : ((-b), a - b) = (a, b)) : a = 0 ∧ b = 0 := by
  simp only [Prod.mk.injEq] at h; omega

/-- Fox 3-colourings of the trefoil diagram (arcs x y z; at each crossing 2·over = under₁ + under₂ mod 3): exactly 9. -/
def trefoilOK (x y z : Fin 3) : Bool :=
  (2 * x.val) % 3 == (y.val + z.val) % 3 && (2 * y.val) % 3 == (x.val + z.val) % 3 && (2 * z.val) % 3 == (x.val + y.val) % 3
theorem trefoil_nine_colourings :
    ((List.finRange 3).flatMap fun x => (List.finRange 3).flatMap fun y => (List.finRange 3).filter fun z => trefoilOK x y z).length = 9 := by
  decide

/-- Trivalent counting (Mirzakhani's pants): a pants decomposition of a genus-g surface with n boundaries has
    2g−2+n pants and 3g−3+n curves; each pants has 3 cuffs, each curve is shared by 2 pants, each boundary by 1. -/
theorem pants_trivalent (g n : Int) : 3 * (2 * g - 2 + n) = 2 * (3 * g - 3 + n) + n := by omega

/-- So the number of cutting curves is ≡ n (mod 3). -/
theorem pants_curves_mod3 (g n : Int) : (3 * g - 3 + n) % 3 = n % 3 := by omega

/-- The same identity for planar cubic (Tr φ³) trees with n legs: n−2 vertices, n−3 propagators. -/
theorem cubic_tree_trivalent (n : Int) : 3 * (n - 2) = 2 * (n - 3) + n := by omega

# SCRIPT: SM369-KNOTS — Alexander polynomial (Fox calculus on the Wirtinger presentation) and Fox p-colourings from PD codes
import snappy, sympy as sp
t = sp.symbols('t')
def arcs_of(pd):
    edges = sorted({e for c in pd for e in c}); parent = {e: e for e in edges}
    def f(x):
        while parent[x] != x: parent[x] = parent[parent[x]]; x = parent[x]
        return x
    for a, b, c, d in pd: parent[f(b)] = f(d)          # over-strand edges b, d are one arc
    roots = sorted({f(e) for e in edges}); idx = {r: i for i, r in enumerate(roots)}
    return (lambda e: idx[f(e)]), len(roots)
def alexander(name):
    L = snappy.Link(name); pd = L.PD_code(); A, n = arcs_of(pd); signs = [cr.sign for cr in L.crossings]
    M = sp.zeros(len(pd), n)
    for r, ((a, b, c, d), s) in enumerate(zip(pd, signs)):
        k, i, j = A(b), A(a), A(c)
        if s > 0: M[r, k] += 1 - t; M[r, i] += t; M[r, j] += -1
        else:     M[r, k] += 1 - t; M[r, i] += -1; M[r, j] += t
    D = sp.factor(sp.expand(M[1:, 1:].det()))
    P = sp.Poly(sp.expand(D), t); cs = P.all_coeffs()
    while cs and cs[-1] == 0: cs = cs[:-1]
    if cs and cs[0] < 0: cs = [-x for x in cs]
    return cs, pd, A, n
def colourings(pd, A, n, p):
    # Fox colouring: 2*over - under_in - under_out = 0 mod p ; count = p^(n - rank_p)
    M = sp.zeros(len(pd), n)
    for r, (a, b, c, d) in enumerate(pd): M[r, A(b)] += 2; M[r, A(a)] -= 1; M[r, A(c)] -= 1
    rk = sp.Matrix(M).applyfunc(lambda x: x % p)
    # rank over GF(p)
    from sympy import GF
    from sympy.polys.matrices import DomainMatrix
    dm = DomainMatrix.from_Matrix(rk).convert_to(GF(p))
    return p ** (n - dm.rank())
