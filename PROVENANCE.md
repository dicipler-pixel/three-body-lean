# Provenance

Jeromie Beasley wrote *The Three-Body Problem, Operator-First*, his second paper,
and assembled the manuscript primarily in Overleaf by hand, selecting and
editing material from his AI chats. This repository contains the later Lean
formalization. The 3-6-9 report is a separate, later study.

`ThreeBody/Basic.lean` and the three files in `FalseControls/` were written for this repository
from *The Three-Body Problem, Operator-First* (v3, 22 August 2026, DOI
10.5281/zenodo.20764188): Eq. (1), Theorems 1, 5, 9 and 10, Propositions 3, 4, 8 and 17, and
Remark 7.
